EPHI Admin - Issue New Certificate

Information for travelers

This digital certificate serves as official proof of an individual's travel vaccination status including Yellow Fever vaccination, as required under the International Health Regulations (IHR 2005).

According to IHR guidelines, the Certificate of Vaccination against Yellow Fever becomes valid 10 days after the date of vaccination and remains valid for the lifetime of the vaccinated individual.

To obtain a new digital copy of your certificate or to verify its authenticity, visit ephi.gov.et and follow the instructions.
