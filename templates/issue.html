Information for travelers

This digital certificate serves as official proof of an individual's travel vaccination status including Yellow Fever vaccination, as required under the International Health Regulations (IHR 2005).

According to IHR guidelines, the Certificate of Vaccination against Yellow Fever becomes valid 10 days after the date of vaccination and remains valid for the lifetime of the vaccinated individual.

Country-specific vaccination certificate requirements are published by the World Health Organization (WHO) in the annual guide Vaccination Requirements and Health Advice for International Travel.

For the latest travel health alerts, to locate designated Yellow Fever vaccination centers in Ethiopia, or to learn more about travel-related health services, please visit the Ethiopian Public Health Institute (EPHI) official website: https://ephi.gov.et

To obtain a new digital copy of your certificate or to verify its authenticity, visit and follow the instructions provided.
